Oceanography and Seafloor Mapping

Super excited at the prospect of applying my knowledge in oceanography and GIS in the same context. Understanding the world we live in is integral to knowing how to properly use and conserve our resources, especially when it comes to fragile seafloor ecosystems. By documenting the unique structures and bathymetry of these areas, we can help identify if, when, and where it's appropriate to gather rare and valuable resources like nickel and cobalt.

Only 20% of the seafloor has been mapped at a high resolution. Assisting in discovering and capturing new places would benefit not just potential mining operations, but also scientists and governmental agencies. Sharing new discoveries with the public through social media also inspires young scientists and allows people to connect with the environment around them.
