Oceanography and Seafloor Mapping

Super excited at the prospect of applying my knowledge in oceanography and GIS in the same context. Understanding the world we live in is integral to knowing how to properly use and conserve our resources, especially when it comes to fragile seafloor ecosystems. By documenting the unique structures and bathymetry of these areas, we can help identify when and where it's appropriate to gather rare and valuable resources like nickel and cobalt.
