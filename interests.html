Oceanography and Seafloor Mapping

Super excited at the prospect of applying my knowledge in oceanography and GIS in the same context. Understanding the world we live in is integral to knowing how to properly use and conserve our resources, especially when it comes to fragile and unfamiliar seafloor ecosystems.

Only 20% of the seafloor has been mapped at a high resolution. So much exists to be discovered! These new finds benefit not just oceanographers, but also historians, biologists, chemists, geologists, and the global public. New discoveries excite and inspire the next generation of scientists. Especially with the help of social media and video streaming, sharing these finds is more straightforward than ever.
