About

My favorite coursework typically centers around applying GIS in different contexts and understanding biogeochemical oceanic processes. I like to do a little bit of everything and I enjoy learning new skills. Teamwork and real-world impact are things that are important to me.

Practicing my data analysis skills investigating environmental processes (earthquake magnitude trends, ocean temperatures) and writing and knitting .rmd files
Cleaning CSV files and entering new material into the diet database
Learning about hydrothermal vents, nutrient cycling, and a bit of wave physics in my oceanography course

Alongside my coursework, I also enjoy hiking and travelling. I studied sustainable energy systems in Thailand this summer and had the opportunity to hear from many different professionals and community leaders. One experience that has stayed especially close with me was our discussion with Goldman Environmental Prize winner and activist Niwat Roykaew, also known as Kru Thi. His wisdom and passion for his community has strengthened my stewardship and continuously reminds me that even when things look grim, it's always worth it to try.
